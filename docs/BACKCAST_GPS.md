# Backcasting GPS for RailScout

Backcasting GPS converts upgraded thought into execution. It begins at the desired end state, works backward to the present, then produces the smallest immediate action that moves the bottleneck.

## Purpose

RailScout must not leave refined ideas as abstract strategy. Every serious idea must be forced into lawful, testable existence through staged action, evidence capture, and review.

"Force into existence" means: convert thought into an authorized artifact, experiment, outreach packet, validation sprint, launch packet, decision record, prototype, operating rule, or next action. It does not mean bypassing law, consent, evidence, budget, governance, or human approval.

## GPS sequence

### 1. Destination

Define the end state in measurable terms.

Required fields:

- Desired outcome
- Target user or participant
- Business mechanism
- Proof, trust, eligibility, routing, settlement, compliance, or coordination failure being fixed
- Measurable win condition
- Deadline or review horizon

### 2. Current position

Describe the present reality without theater.

Required fields:

- What exists now
- What is missing
- What evidence exists
- What evidence does not exist
- Current constraints
- Known risks
- Unknowns that control the decision

### 3. Route

Work backward from the destination into stages.

Required fields:

- Milestones
- Projects
- Tasks
- Next actions
- Dependencies
- Kill criteria
- Rollback path
- Fastest falsification test

### 4. Single Bottleneck Metric

Name the one metric most responsible for progress.

Examples:

- verified buyer pain interviews completed;
- qualified pilot commitments obtained;
- opportunity packets completed to standard;
- falsification tests run;
- launch packets ready for outbound;
- external responses from target participants;
- validated willingness to pay;
- time from raw signal to ranked opportunity packet.

Every plan must tie actions to the active Single Bottleneck Metric.

### 5. Execution card

End with one immediate execution card:

- **Do now:** the next action.
- **Owner:** who acts.
- **Evidence produced:** what artifact or proof will exist afterward.
- **Time box:** when to review.
- **Stop condition:** what makes the action invalid.
- **Next gate:** what must be true before advancing.

## RailScout default business backcast

### Destination

RailScout becomes a real digital business that repeatedly discovers, designs, falsifies, ranks, validates, and helps build Category-One infrastructure businesses.

### Win condition

RailScout can produce a complete launch-ready opportunity packet from raw signal to ranked execution plan, including evidence, falsification tests, stakeholder map, wedge design, commercial thesis, risks, and next action.

### Required milestone chain

1. Canonical doctrine and workstation protocol exist.
2. Founder-intent ledger preserves material operating instructions.
3. Research refinery loop turns raw input into opportunity packets.
4. Falsification sprint template kills or advances ideas quickly.
5. Ranking rubric prioritizes commercial upside, moat potential, capital efficiency, downside protection, and speed to proof.
6. Launch packet factory produces outreach, landing page copy, pilot offer, validation script, data-room outline, and decision record.
7. External validation loop records real responses, negative evidence, and revisions.
8. Repeatable service becomes software-supported refinery.
9. Validated service compounds into RailScout's own business infrastructure.

### Active Single Bottleneck Metric

Completed opportunity packets that reach a real external validation attempt.

A beautiful internal artifact that never reaches a participant is not enough. The refinery must produce proof-seeking outputs.
